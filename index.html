The 3×3×3 Paradigm

The fundamental DNA of the QUBE-ISO system is standardizing volume to maximize spatial efficiency and structural predictability.

X

3m Width

Standardized bay width allows for predictable facade panelling, interchangeable interior partition walls, and optimal daylight penetration ratios.

Y

3m Depth

The structural grid extends longitudinally in 3-meter increments, creating highly divisible leaseable spaces perfectly suited for micro-commercial or modular residential use.

Z

3m Height

Floor-to-floor height set precisely at 3 meters. This allows for a 2.6m clear acoustic ceiling height with a dedicated 400mm plenum for decentralized MEP and HVAC routing.

Technical Specifications

Detailed system breakdown of the parametric core.

Architectural Strategy

  • Leaseable Space Allocation Configured in raw 9m² (3x3) blocks. Units can be combined seamlessly without load-bearing interruptions. Marked as the orange zones in the master plan.
  • Vertical & Horizontal Circulation Centralized lift lobbies (Grey) serve as the primary artery. Egress and secondary vertical circulation utilize decentralized stair cores (Red) situated at the perimeter wings.
  • Facade & Envelope Standardized 3m-wide modular exterior panels. Allows for rapid assembly of glazing, solid insulation panels, or passive shading fins depending on orientation.

Structural Matrix

  • Parametric Grid (3000mm) Rigid 3m x 3m column layout utilizing reinforced concrete or steel composite members. Ensures universal load distribution and eliminates the need for expensive transfer slabs.
  • Floor Slabs Pre-cast or post-tensioned 200mm slabs optimized for the 3m span. Minimal deflection allows for varied loading scenarios from light commercial to heavy storage.
  • Core Stability Lateral wind and seismic forces are resisted by the central lift core and the rigid perimeter stairwell enclosures (acting as shear walls).

MEP, Electrical & HVAC

  • Centralized Shafts (Tan Zone) Primary wet lines (plumbing) and heavy mechanical exhaust are routed through dedicated shafts immediately adjacent to the central lobby for easy maintenance access.
  • Plug-and-Play HVAC Decentralized VRF systems or chilled water branches are distributed within the 400mm ceiling plenum. Each 3x3 block has standardized punch-out points in the framing for easy ducting.
  • Electrical Grid Distribution Busbar systems run along the main corridors. Each leaseable module taps into this grid directly, allowing individual sub-metering and scalable power drops without rewiring the building core.
